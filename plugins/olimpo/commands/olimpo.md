Follow `olimpiada`. Discover tools with `system.searchTools` ("olimpo").

- `practice [topic]`: use `study_plan` or `problem_random`; statement, attempt,
  hints on request, solution last.
- `explain <id|technique>`: retrieve and explain one step at a time, respecting
  `solutionType` and provenance.
- `search <text>`: use `problem_search` and list IDs, titles and topics.
- No arguments: propose a mixed study plan.

Accept the legacy input aliases `practica`, `explica`, and `busca` for compatibility.

This is not an official IMO archive. Short-answer matching does not grade proofs
or symbolic equivalence. Imported material is user-provided/unreviewed.
