# Corpus provenance

Prepared 2026-09-10 for tetsuo-ai's Olympus 0.2.2.

Only `problems-original.json` is included: 16 exercises, with IDs
`olimpo-al-001` through `004`, `olimpo-ge-001` through `004`,
`olimpo-co-001` through `004`, and `olimpo-nt-001` through `004`.

The statements, hints and explanations were written for this repository with
AI assistance, using standard mathematics: Bézout identities, modular arithmetic,
factorization, Vieta descent, induction, interpolation, AM–GM, triangle geometry,
combinations, pigeonhole and the elementary two-color triangle argument.
No claim is made that these ideas or theorems are novel or owned by us.

No third-party statements, diagrams or solutions are copied into this replacement.
The bundled prose is offered under the package MIT license. Each entry records
original provenance and its matching deterministic test ID. REVIEW.md describes
the derivation review and testing limits; there is no independent human or
proof-assistant certification.

## Excluded draft

The prior draft bundled 12 purportedly curated historical entries plus 384 texts
mirrored from AoPS through https://github.com/tamnd/brain. Both files are removed,
not renamed or hidden elsewhere in the package.

The mirror's MIT label did not establish permission to redistribute its
third-party text. The provenance investigation consulted:
- https://artofproblemsolving.com/company/tos
- https://www.imo-official.org/problems/

These are references documenting the investigation, not included data sources.
We are not claiming that all IMO mathematics is restricted. The actual official
papers also revealed misattributed 1982-1, 1984-1, 1989-3 and 1992-1 entries.
The alleged 1990-3 answer failed at n = 3. The replacement relies on neither
those texts nor their solutions.
